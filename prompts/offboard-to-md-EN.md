# Collider — Self-Offboard to Markdown

Copy the prompt below into the AI assistant that knows you best.

```text
You are creating my COLLIDER OFFBOARD: a compact, self-contained Markdown profile that another person — or another AI — can read before we meet.

The goal is not a CV, biography, personality test, or sales pitch. The goal is to transfer enough useful context that a group can start with shared knowledge instead of spending the first hour reconstructing who everyone is.

Use what you already know about me from this conversation and any memory/context you legitimately have access to. Do not invent missing facts. If something important is missing, either omit it or mark it as "Not enough context".

Write in my language unless I ask otherwise.

IMPORTANT:
- Return ONLY Markdown.
- No preamble, no explanation after it.
- Make it useful to humans first, but regular enough for an LLM to parse.
- Keep it compact: roughly 700–1200 words unless there is genuinely less to say.
- Prefer concrete examples, projects, tensions, odd interests, and working patterns over generic adjectives.
- Preserve my own vocabulary when it is distinctive.
- Do not include passwords, API keys, private addresses, account numbers, sensitive health data, or information I would reasonably not want shared with a new group.
- Clearly separate facts you know from interpretations. Do not fabricate confidence.
- The result must stand alone: a reader should not need access to this chat.

Use EXACTLY this structure:

# COLLIDER OFFBOARD — [Name]

> **In one line:** [A useful, specific description of me — not a slogan.]

## Who I am
A short paragraph: current context, role/domain, and the arc that brought me here.

## What I actually do
- [3–7 concrete things I spend energy on]
- Include how this differs from my formal title if relevant.

## What I know / can help with
### Deep
- [areas where I have substantial practical depth]

### Useful
- [areas where I can contribute, connect dots, or help move work forward]

### Curious about
- [areas I am actively learning or exploring]

## What I am working on now
For each important current project or theme:
- **[Name]:** what it is, why I care, and current status in 1–3 sentences.

## How I think
- **Mental models:** [recurring frameworks, analogies, or ways I decompose problems]
- **Default move:** [what I tend to do when a problem is fuzzy]
- **Evidence style:** [what tends to convince me]
- **Tension I keep returning to:** [a real trade-off or question I care about]

## How I work with people
- **Best with:** [conditions / collaboration patterns]
- **I tend to bring:** [what others can expect from me]
- **Please do:** [ways to work well with me]
- **Please avoid:** [things that create friction]
- **Communication:** [direct/async/visual/iterative/etc., grounded in observed behavior]

## Things that light me up
- [specific topics, problems, tools, domains, or kinds of conversation]
- Include at least one non-work interest if known.

## Things that drain me
- [specific situations, not personality labels]

## My current edges
Things I am trying to get better at, unresolved questions, constraints, or areas where I want help.
- [3–6 items]

## Unexpected / human stuff
- [3–7 memorable details, hobbies, places, quirks, side projects, or references that make connection easier]
- Keep this shareable and non-sensitive.

## If we are about to work together
**You can pull me in when:** [specific situations]

**I would love to meet people who:** [specific kinds of people, expertise, or perspectives]

**Ask me about:** [5–8 strong conversation starters]

## Current context snapshot
- **Generated:** [YYYY-MM-DD]
- **Source:** AI-assisted self-offboard based on available conversation/context
- **Confidence note:** [briefly identify any sections where context was thin or possibly stale]
- **Sharing:** Intended to be shared with the Collider group; exclude anything that should not travel.

End after the final bullet. Do not add commentary.
```

The returned Markdown is the artifact. Save it as `firstname-lastname.md` and bring it into the shared Collider space.
